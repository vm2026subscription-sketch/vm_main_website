Common questions

Before you apply

Quick answers to the questions students ask most about entrance exams.

Which entrance exam should I take after Class 12?

It depends on your stream and career goal. Engineering aspirants usually take JEE Main or their state CET, medical aspirants take NEET UG, law aspirants take CLAT or AILET, and design, management and defence students have dedicated exams. Open the guide for your target course to compare eligibility and dates.

Where can I find the latest exam dates?

Each exam guide links to the official conducting body. Always verify application, admit card and result dates on the official website or our Exam Updates section, because dates can change every year.

Are previous year question papers available?

Yes. Exam guides list the previous year paper coverage we have, with a link to download or practise the papers. Solve them under timed conditions and review every mistake.

Can I apply for more than one entrance exam?

Yes, most candidates apply for multiple exams. Check each notification for eligibility, exam dates and counselling rules, and avoid clashes in your final schedule.

What documents are needed for applications?

Commonly required documents include a photograph, signature, Class 10 and 12 marksheets, category or income certificate, ID proof and, for some courses, a domicile certificate. Keep scanned copies ready before the application window opens.
